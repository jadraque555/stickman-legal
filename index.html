My YouTube Uploader

My YouTube Uploader is a private, personal automation tool. It prepares short explainer videos and uploads them, as scheduled or private videos, to YouTube channels that belong to the person who runs it.

It is not a public service, it has no sign-up, and it is not offered to other users. The only person who ever signs in to it is its owner, to connect the owner's own YouTube channels.

What it asks Google for

One permission: youtube.upload, which lets the app upload videos to the channel the owner chose. It cannot read the owner's email, contacts, files or any other Google data, and it cannot manage or delete existing videos.
