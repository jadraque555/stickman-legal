Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

My YouTube Uploader is a private tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. This policy explains what the tool does with Google user data.

Data the app accesses

When the owner connects a YouTube channel, Google asks for one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload (upload videos to YouTube). The app uses it only to upload the owner's videos and to set their title, description, tags, thumbnail and schedule. The app does not read the owner's email address, profile, contacts, Drive files or any other Google data.

How the data is stored

Google gives the app an access token and a refresh token. They are stored in a protected file on the computer or server run by the owner, and nowhere else. They are not sent to the app's developer, to any third party, or to any analytics or advertising service.

Sharing and selling

The app does not share, sell, transfer or disclose Google user data to anyone. It contains no advertising and no analytics. The videos it uploads are the owner's own content.

Use of Google data

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete the stored tokens by deleting the file where they are kept. Revoking access stops all uploads immediately. Videos already uploaded stay on YouTube until the owner deletes them there.

Changes and contact
